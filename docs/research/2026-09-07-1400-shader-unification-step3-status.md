## Implemented authority and closure

`render_rtxdi.h` now embeds `rtxdi_di.slang`. `RenderRTXDI::initialize` at
`render_rtxdi.cpp:59` uses the existing grouped ShaderRD initializer with row-major
default and no vertex invariant decoration. The unchanged host registry, snapshots,
TLAS, viewport resources, descriptors, reservoir rotation and teardown retain their
owners. No new host table, public API, material support or rendering feature was added.

Paths below are relative to `servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/`:

- `surface_data_inc.slang:52`, `decode_surface_buffers`, extends the STEP2 shared
  semantic/packing contract. The six existing exports remain unchanged; motion,
  linear depth and current/previous history stay in the DI wrapper.
- `raytracing/surface_brdf_inc.slang:336,340,348` owns evaluation, PDF and sampling
  for `SurfaceData`. The active GGX/Lambert closure replaces the old selectable BRDF
  file and its language aliases; these functions do not depend on RAB or SDK RNG.
- `raytracing/light_data_inc.slang` and `light_sampling_inc.slang` retain the
  192-byte light ABI, analytic/cone/projector/area/emissive/environment proposals,
  emission sampling and solid-angle PDFs. Emissive history selects previous
  deformation positions through the current geometry record and old light transform.
- `raytracing/geometry_data_inc.slang` owns geometry flags and the existing data
  layouts. `geometry_decode_inc.slang:47,68,185` owns checked CLAS remap/index/UV/
  position/color decoding and explicit footprint derivatives. `GeometryHitInput`

  replaced by an argument. Queries package their builtin results into explicit inputs.
- `raytracing/rtxdi_application_bridge_inc.slang:39,108,135` retains camera/history
  adaptation, checked light-index translation, SDK callbacks and reservoir sampling
  metadata; it delegates renderer shading and visibility to the shared library.

## Validation boundary and STEP4 handoff

This establishes native source compilation, include embedding, static host/layout
agreement, final SPIR-V validity and real-device shader/pipeline creation. It does
not establish DI dispatch, descriptor binding with scene resources, numerical/image
equivalence, barriers, visibility output, camera cuts, deformation, resize, export,
template or separate rendering-thread behavior. No automated tests or numerical
assertions were authored or run. Full editor/template builds and ordinary real-scene
rendering remain STEP5 requirements after STEP4 closes the deliberately broken graph.

Dormant `scene_raytracing_raygen.glsl` still refers to deleted BRDF helpers at
117/274, hit helpers at 281/484 and data at 589; `raytracing_lights_inc.glsl:4`
refers to the deleted light helper. `effects/rtxdi_frame.glsl:12` refers to the
deleted handwritten NRD include. These are explicit intermediate dependency breaks,
not retained compatibility paths. STEP4 removes the dormant pipeline/bundle/readiness
machinery and migrates frame consumers; no dormant PT shader was ported to hide this.

Canvas/UI, sky/fog/particles and other distinct upstream GLSL consumers were not
migrated. D3D12/Metal RT remains excluded by the approved plan; their code and public
GLSL interfaces were untouched. This step does not claim backend parity for RT.

ShaderRD, RenderRTXDI, host geometry structs and Vulkan feature initialization.
One-shot RenderRTXDI references were TU-local, not a complete caller inventory.
